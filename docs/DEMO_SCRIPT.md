# Demo script (under 3 minutes, one pass)

Pilot records and narrates in their own voice. Tone: respectful, about organisations' patterns, not individuals' tragedies. Times are targets. The exact clicks and addresses are in docs/VIDEO_SHOT_LIST.md.

Every figure on screen is read from the database by the page. Read the figures off the screen rather than from this script, so the voice always matches the picture.

## Before recording

1. Start the site, then open a private browser window at 1280 by 800, light mode, zoom 110%.
2. Hide the guided path strip once (the cross on the right), so it is not in the shots. A private window forgets this when closed.
3. Add ?replay to an address whenever you want its animation to play again. The home timeline and the map intro otherwise play once per session.
4. Practise once. Each animation takes under 8 seconds and ends on a still frame, so there is time to speak over it.

## 0:00 to 0:35. The problem

On screen: the home page. The smart motorway timeline draws itself, report by report, and the coroner counter ticks up. Scroll to the agent's recorded run, then to the 100 dots.

Say:

1. "A coroner warns an organisation about a risk to life. It responds with what it will do."
2. "Years later the same concern comes up again, in front of a different coroner. Here, seven coroners, and none cited the earlier report."
3. Over the dots: "Across every link we found, the later report almost never cites the earlier one, and most came from a different coroner. Recurrence Watch connects the record."

## 0:35 to 1:00. One chain

On screen: "See a repeated warning". The story reveals as you scroll; long gaps take longer to draw. Open "Check it in the source" on one quote.

Say:

1. "One hazard, one organisation, report by report, with what was promised in between."
2. As the words light up: "Every quote is checked by code, word for word, against the original report, and links to it."

## 1:00 to 1:40. Watch the agent

On screen: Check a report. Press the "A smart motorway death, 2023" chip. The recorded trace replays: model badges, ticking token and cost counters, verified marks on kept quotes, dropped verdicts fading with their reason.

Say:

1. "Give it any report. An agent built on NVIDIA Nemotron, on Nebius Token Factory, decides what to do next."
2. "Nano reads, Qwen3 embeddings search the organisation's earlier warnings, Super judges each candidate. Every kept quote is verified in code."
3. Point to the cost counter: "This run cost under a cent."

## 1:40 to 2:05. You be the judge

On screen: You be the judge. Start, answer one round, watch the verdict turn over, then stop.

Say:

1. "Same hazard or not? Make the call yourself, then see what Nemotron Super decided, and why."
2. On the end screen: "Reading every pair this way would take a person days. The model judged them all for the cost shown."

## 2:05 to 2:25. The map

On screen: Hazard map. The intro zooms to the smart motorway chain and draws its lines, then hands the map back. Hover a point to light its chain.

Say:

1. "Every concern in the archive, placed by meaning. The lines are recurrences the judge confirmed."

## 2:25 to 2:50. How we know

On screen: How we know. The cost rolls up; What surprised us; scroll the funnel as each step narrows with its cost beside it. Then the Evaluation tab.

Say:

1. "From concerns extracted to verified chains, each step with what it cost on Token Factory."
2. On Evaluation: "On recurrences that coroners themselves flagged, the Nemotron judge found the most at the same number of links, and we show exactly where it fails."

## 2:50 to 3:00. Close

On screen: back to the home page.

Say:

1. "For coroners, families, lawyers and safety teams."
2. "PFD Monitor groups concerns. The Tracker chases deadlines. Recurrence Watch tests whether promises held."
